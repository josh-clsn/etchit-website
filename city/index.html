browse the >city.

An index of pages, books, music, video, and games that live on the Autonomi network. Click any entry — fetch>it picks it up by hash and renders it on your device. No DNS, no proxy in the middle, no account, no fee for the fetch. Browse before you install; install once and every link below just works.

checking… Don't have fetch>it yet? Pick the one that matches your platform. On Windows / macOS / Linux you need both: the desktop app (does the actual fetching) and the browser extension (catches autonomi:// clicks here and routes them to the app — without it, browsers silently block the scheme). iOS is in private beta — limited slots; email hello@etchit.io if you want one. Safari isn't supported yet.
